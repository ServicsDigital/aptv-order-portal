Android Smart TV Installation Guide

📺 Android Smart TV Setup

Follow these steps to set up your compatible Android Smart TV and install your authorized application.

Step 1 — Connect Your TV to the Internet

Open your TV's Settings → Network menu and connect the television to your Wi-Fi network.

Step 2 — Open Google Play Store

From your Android TV home screen, open the Google Play Store.

Step 3 — Find Your Authorized App

Search for the application you have been instructed to use. Install it from its official or authorized source.

Step 4 — Launch the App

Once installation is complete, select Open or find the application in your TV's app list.

Step 5 — Sign In

Enter the account or login information provided with your authorized service.

🔐 Account Information

Keep your login information private and only use credentials provided for your authorized account.

Important: Only install applications from legitimate, authorized sources and use services you are authorized to access.
