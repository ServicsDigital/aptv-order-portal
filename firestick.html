Firestick Installation Guide

🔥 Firestick / Fire TV Setup

Follow the steps below to prepare your Firestick for the APTV app.

1

Open Settings

From your Firestick home screen, select the Settings option.

2

Open My Fire TV

Select My Fire TV from the settings menu.

3

Open Developer Options

Select Developer Options. If your Firestick shows "About" instead, select your Fire TV device several times until Developer Options becomes available.

4

Install Downloader

Return to the Firestick home screen and search for the Downloader app. Install the official Downloader app.

5

Open Downloader

Open Downloader and allow the permissions it requests. Use the official APTV installation information provided by Arena Pulse TV.

6

Install the APTV App

Follow the on-screen instructions to download and install the APTV application.

7

Open APTV

Once installation is complete, open the APTV application and follow the login or activation instructions provided with your service.

⚠️ Important

Only use installation information supplied through your official Arena Pulse TV customer instructions. Never enter codes or download files from unknown sources.
