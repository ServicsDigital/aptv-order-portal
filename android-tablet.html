Android Tablet Installation Guide

📱 Android Tablet Setup

Follow these steps to install and set up your authorized APTV application on a compatible Android tablet.

Step 1 — Connect to Wi-Fi

Open Settings → Network & Internet and make sure your Android tablet is connected to the internet.

Step 2 — Open Google Play Store

Open the Google Play Store on your Android tablet.

Step 3 — Find Your Authorized App

Search for the application you have been instructed to use. Install it from its official or authorized source.

Step 4 — Open the App

After installation, tap Open or launch the application from your tablet's app list.

Step 5 — Sign In

Enter the account or login information provided with your authorized service.

🔐 Account Information

Keep your login information private and only use credentials provided for your authorized account.

Important: Only install applications from legitimate, authorized sources and use services you are authorized to access.

🆘 Need Help?

Having trouble getting your Android tablet set up? Contact APTV support for assistance.
