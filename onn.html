ONN 4K Pro / Android TV Installation Guide

📺 ONN 4K Pro Setup

Follow the steps below to prepare your ONN 4K Pro or compatible Android TV device for app installation.

Step 1 — Connect Your ONN Device

Connect the ONN 4K Pro to your television using HDMI and complete the initial Android TV setup.

Step 2 — Connect to Wi-Fi

From the Android TV home screen, open Settings → Network & Internet and connect your device to your Wi-Fi network.

Step 3 — Open Google Play Store

From the home screen, open the Google Play Store.

Step 4 — Install Your Authorized App

Search for the app you have been instructed to install and install it from its official or authorized source.

Step 5 — Open the App

Return to the Android TV home screen, open Your Apps, and launch the newly installed app.

🔐 Account Setup

Enter the account or login information provided with your authorized service.

Important: Only install applications from legitimate, authorized sources and use services you are authorized to access.

🆘 Need Help?

If you have trouble completing the setup, contact APTV support for assistance.
